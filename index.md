# Respite Support

Respite is a calm intermittent fasting timer for iPhone and Apple Watch. Start a fast, see how long is left, and keep track of your history and streak. No account, no ads, and your data stays with you.

## Contact
Questions, problems or refund help: **batuna07@gmail.com**

## Restoring Respite Lifetime
Respite Lifetime is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. On a new or reset device, open Respite, go to **Settings** and tap **Restore Purchases**, while signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

## Your fasts and iCloud
Your fasts and weight entries are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also kept in your own private iCloud storage so they come back on a new iPhone and stay in sync across your devices. We can't see this data. **Settings → Your Data** shows whether iCloud Sync is on.

## Apple Watch
The Apple Watch app and its complications are part of Respite Lifetime. Open Respite on your iPhone once so the watch can connect. Fasts you start or end on the watch are saved on your iPhone.

## Siri, Shortcuts and Control Center
Say "Start a fast in Respite" or "End my fast in Respite", or add the **Fasting** control to Control Center, the Lock Screen or the Action button.

## Health note
Respite is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Talk to a healthcare professional before starting a fasting routine, especially if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have diabetes or another medical condition, take medication, or have a history of disordered eating.
